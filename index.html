How it works

From production error to shipped fix.

Glue follows the investigation from the moment something breaks to the moment you're ready to ship the fix. It gathers the context, traces the likely cause, and prepares the work for you. You stay in control the entire time.

Step 1

Step 3

You're always in control. Glue never merges without your approval.

Questions you might have

Is this just another AI coding assistant?

No. AI coding assistants help you write code. Glue starts with the production incident. It gathers the context around what broke, checks recent changes, traces the likely cause, and prepares a fix for you to review.

What about tools like Sentry Seer or Copilot Workspace?

Those are useful tools. Glue is focused on connecting the investigation across your existing workflow, from the production error to the related code and changes. The goal isn't to replace every tool you use, it's to reduce the time spent jumping between them.

Can Glue push directly to production?

No. Glue prepares a draft pull request. You review the changes and decide when or whether to merge.

Does Glue replace developers?

Definitely not. Glue handles repetitive investigation work. You still understand the problem, review the solution, and make the final decision.
